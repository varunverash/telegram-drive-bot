Privacy Policy — Telegram Music Bot

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Telegram Music Bot is a personal automation bot that transfers files sent through Telegram to the owner's Google Drive.

Information the bot processes

When a user sends a file to the bot, the bot may process:

- Telegram user ID
- Telegram message information
- File name
- File type
- File size
- The file itself

This information is processed only as necessary to receive, identify, transfer, organize, and store files in the owner's Google Drive.

How files are stored

Files sent to the bot may be uploaded to the owner's Google Drive.

The bot does not intentionally make uploaded files publicly available. Access to the stored files is controlled by the Google Drive account that owns them.

Google Drive

The bot uses the Google Drive API to upload and manage files.

The bot uses OAuth authentication to access the owner's Google Drive. It does not request or intentionally access unrelated Google account information.

Telegram

The bot receives information provided through Telegram when users interact with it. Telegram's own privacy practices and terms also apply to the use of Telegram.

How information is used

Information processed by the bot is used for purposes such as:

- Receiving files from Telegram
- Uploading files to Google Drive
- Detecting duplicate files
- Organizing files
- Tracking upload status
- Managing bot operations

The bot does not intentionally sell personal information or use the information processed by the bot for advertising.

Data retention

Files uploaded through the bot may remain in the owner's Google Drive until they are deleted by the owner or through the bot's file-management functions.

Bot operational information may also be retained as necessary for the bot to track uploads and avoid duplicate processing.

Sharing of information

The bot does not intentionally sell or rent user information.

Information may be processed by third-party services required for the bot to operate, including Telegram, Google Drive, GitHub Actions, and related infrastructure.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the bot's credentials and stored information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

The bot is not specifically directed toward children.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.
